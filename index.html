Wisconsin

Wisconsin is a state in the midwestern United States. It is bounded by Lake Michigan to the east, Illinois to the south, Iowa and Minnesota to the west, and Lake Superior and Michigan to the north. Known as America's Dairyland, Wisconsin is famous for its cheese, dairy production, beer, and manufacturing. The state features thousands of glacial lakes, rolling hills, and scenic forests. Madison is the state capital, and Milwaukee is its largest city.
